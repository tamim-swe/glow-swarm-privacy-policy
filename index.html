Welcome to Glow Swarm ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). We are dedicated to providing an enjoyable, transparent, and secure gaming experience. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you install, play, and interact with Glow Swarm on Android devices.

1. 100% Offline Gameplay & Local Data Storage

  • No Account Required: Glow Swarm does not require any account registration, email sign-up, login credentials, or personal profiles.
  • Local On-Device Storage: All game progression—including your unlocked levels (1–30), earned Glow Coins, swarm evolution tiers (Firefly, Dragonfly, Phoenix), purchased shop upgrades (Start Swarm Size, Income Multiplier, Magnet Range), unlocked glowing skins, high scores, daily streaks, and stars—is stored exclusively and locally on your physical device using local Hive storage.
  • We do not operate remote user databases or backend servers to store your personal identity.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

Glow Swarm is free to play and is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC).

A. Types of Advertisements

  1. Rewarded Video Ads (Optional & User-Initiated): You may voluntarily choose to watch a short video ad in exchange for in-game rewards (such as 2X coins at level completion, a single revive after a game-over, opening a Free Chest early, an extra Daily Lucky Spin, or discounted shop upgrades). Rewards are granted only after the ad has finished playing.
  2. Interstitial Ads (Full-Screen): Non-intrusive interstitial ads may occasionally appear between level transitions (never in levels 1–3, never during active running gameplay, and strictly restricted to every 3rd completed level with a minimum 90-second cooldown).

B. Data Collected Automatically by Google Mobile Ads SDK

When your device is connected to the internet, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process certain technical, non-personally identifiable device data strictly for advertising and fraud prevention purposes:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and carrier.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location derived from IP address (e.g. country or city level) to serve geographically relevant ads.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Views, clicks, impressions, and ad load performance metrics.
  • Diagnostic Information: Crash logs and performance statistics to maintain SDK stability.

For more information on how Google collects, uses, and safeguards data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Data Safety Information
  • Google Ad Personalization Controls

3. Android System Permissions

Glow Swarm requests only the minimum standard permissions necessary to operate:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used strictly to fetch and display AdMob advertisements when the device is online.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to detect whether your device is connected to Wi-Fi/cellular data, allowing the game to gracefully disable ad buttons when offline without interrupting gameplay.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during gameplay actions (passing math gates, collecting coins, boss hits). Haptic feedback can be toggled on or off at any time in the Main Menu settings.

4. Children's Privacy & Families Policy Compliance

  • Glow Swarm is designed as a family-friendly hyper-casual runner with mental math elements.
  • The game does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
  • Advertisements served via Google AdMob are filtered to exclude mature or inappropriate content, ensuring a safe experience for players of all ages in accordance with Google Play's Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Data Retention & User Data Deletion Instructions

Under Google Play data safety requirements, users have the right to delete all data associated with the app:

  • Because all gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device:
    1. In-App Reset / Storage Clear: You can permanently delete all coins, upgrades, skins, and level progression at any time by going to your device's Android Settings > Apps > Glow Swarm > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage.
    2. Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Glow Swarm from your device permanently removes all locally stored game files and progress immediately.
  • We do not retain any copies of your game progress on external servers.

6. Security of Your Information

We value your trust. All network transmissions initiated by third-party advertising SDKs are secured using modern encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in game features, legal obligations, or Google Play store policies. The latest version will always be accessible from the Google Play Store listing and within our repository with an updated "Last Updated" date.
