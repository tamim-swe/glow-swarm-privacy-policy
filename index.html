Privacy Policy for Glow Swarm

Effective Date: October 30, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 01, 2026

Welcome to Glow Swarm ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and shared when you install and play Glow Swarm.

1. Overview & Offline Gameplay

  • Glow Swarm is designed to be fully playable offline with no backend or user account registration required.
  • All game progress, including earned coins, unlocked levels (1–30), swarm tiers, upgrades (Swarm Size, Income Multiplier, Magnet Range), and skins, is stored locally on your device using local storage (Hive).
  • We do not host our own servers, do not require login credentials, and do not collect your personal contact information (such as your name, email address, or phone number).

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

Glow Swarm integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to deliver non-intrusive banner, rewarded, and interstitial advertisements:

  • Rewarded Advertisements: Optional video ads that you voluntarily choose to watch to double your level-complete coins, revive your swarm once per run, unlock free chests, or spin the daily wheel.
  • Interstitial Advertisements: Full-screen ads shown occasionally between levels (never during active gameplay, never in levels 1–3, and with a minimum 90-second cooldown between ads).

When your device is connected to the internet, Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable technical data, including:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • IP address (used for general geographic location such as country or city)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, carrier)
  • Ad interaction events (views, clicks, and impressions)
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics

For more details on how Google processes and safeguards data, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosure

3. Permissions Requested on Android

Glow Swarm requests the following standard Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used strictly to load AdMob advertisements when online and check network status.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the game to detect whether the device is offline or online so that ad buttons can be gracefully adjusted without disrupting offline gameplay.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g. passing gates, collecting coins, boss hits). Haptics can be toggled off at any time in the main menu.

4. Children’s Privacy

Glow Swarm does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. All advertisements served are configured according to standard family-friendly guidelines through Google AdMob filters. If you believe your child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks, please contact us so we can investigate.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your game data is stored exclusively on your device, you may completely delete all stored game data, high scores, coins, and upgrades at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings > Apps > Glow Swarm > Clear Storage/Data, or simply by uninstalling the application.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published here with an updated "Last Updated" date.
